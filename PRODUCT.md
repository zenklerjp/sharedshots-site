# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The homepage is primarily for people hosting weddings, birthdays, parties, road trips, reunions, festivals, and other group events. They want one place to collect everyone’s photos without chasing separate camera rolls afterward.

Guests are a secondary audience. They receive a code or invite link, join an album, and can contribute from the SharedShots app or directly from the browser.

## Product Purpose

SharedShots gives an event one shared camera roll. A host creates an album and shares a six-character code or link. Guests add photos to the same live gallery, with retro film treatments available in the iPhone app.

The homepage succeeds when an event host understands the product quickly and opens the App Store listing. Guest invite links must continue to send people into the existing join and browser-album flows.

## Positioning

SharedShots combines a live group event album with a shared retro-film look. Guests do not need the app to join or add their photos from the browser.

## Operating Context

Hosts create and manage event albums from SharedShots for iPhone. They share a code, QR code, or link in person or through group chats. Guests join the album, shoot or upload photos, and see the shared gallery fill during the event. Browser guests can add photos without installing the app.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Shared album creation for events.
- Six-character invite codes, links, and QR sharing.
- Live galleries that update as photos arrive.
- Nine retro film looks in the app.
- Likes and comments on shared photos.
- Offline capture with later upload when a connection returns.
- Host controls for join approval and member removal.
- Private album access through an invite code.
- Browser-based joining and photo uploads for guests without the app.
- Free albums, one-time Event Pass options, and a Premium subscription exist. The marketing site must not change or invent plan limits or pricing.
- The app is currently available for iPhone and requires iOS 15.1 or later.
- The App Store listing is the factual source for current product claims, pricing, compatibility, and availability.
- Existing `join.html`, `album.html`, `privacy.html`, and `terms.html` routes must remain available.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: SharedShots.
- Existing app icon and teal brand color are recognizable product assets.
- The app’s visual language pairs teal with warm neutral surfaces and retro-film imagery.
- The voice is direct, social, practical, and lightly playful.
- Primary homepage action: download SharedShots from the App Store.

## Product Principles

1. Make the host’s payoff obvious in the first viewport.
2. Show the shared event roll working instead of relying on broad marketing claims.
3. Keep joining friction low, especially for guests who do not have the app.
4. Treat the app’s retro-film character as a product feature, not decorative nostalgia.
5. Preserve privacy, pricing, and platform claims exactly as supported by current evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The website must support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion and transparency preferences, readable contrast, responsive text scaling, and clear alt text. Motion and imagery must never be required to understand the offer or complete the primary action.
