Before you join

SharedShots is a shared photo album. Here is what that means:

  • Photos you add are uploaded so everyone in the album can see them.
  • Anyone in the album can view, save, and keep their own copy. A copy someone saves stays on their device even if you delete the original.
  • You confirm you have the right to share what you upload, and only you can delete your own photos.
  • Albums do not last forever. They are deleted automatically after a while (3 months on the free plan), so save anything you want to keep.

By tapping “I agree and join” you confirm you have the right to share the photos you upload, you give the other members of this album permission to view, download, and keep them, and you accept our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. SharedShots is not responsible for how members use content once it has been shared.
